🚗 LLD Interview: Car Rental System
Problem Statement

Design a Car Rental System similar to Enterprise or Zoomcar.

A customer should be able to search for available cars, reserve a car for a specific period, pick up the car, return it, and pay the rental amount.

Initial requirements

The system should support:

Multiple rental stores/branches.
Each store can have multiple vehicles.
A vehicle can be available, reserved, rented, or under maintenance.
A customer can search for available vehicles at a store.
A customer can reserve a vehicle for a specific pickup and return date/time.
A reserved vehicle cannot be reserved by another customer for an overlapping period.
The customer can pick up the reserved vehicle.
The customer can return the vehicle.
Rental cost is calculated based on the rental duration and the vehicle's price per day.
The customer can pay the final bill.
Support Cash, Card, and UPI payments.
A reservation can be confirmed, cancelled, or completed.
