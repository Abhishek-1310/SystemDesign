question:- 🏧 ATM — LLD Interview Question

You are asked to design an ATM system using Object-Oriented Design.

Requirements

Design an ATM that supports:

Insert card
Enter PIN
Check account balance
Withdraw cash
Deposit cash
Eject card
ATM behavior
ATM should have different states such as:
No card inserted
Card inserted
PIN verified
Transaction in progress
A user cannot perform transactions before entering the correct PIN.
After 3 incorrect PIN attempts, the card should be blocked.
User cannot withdraw more than:
Their account balance
The cash available in the ATM
ATM should dispense cash using available denominations, e.g. ₹500, ₹200, ₹100.
After completing a transaction, the user should be able to perform another transaction or eject the card.
ATM should not accept transactions if it is out of cash.
